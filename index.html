◄ ABOUT ONCHAIN LIFE ►
ONCHAIN LIFE is a virtual arcade machine to visualize and interact with the ONCHAIN LIFE NFT collection.
Every NFT is a pure random CONWAY'S GAME OF LIFE toroidal grid of 64x64 BLOCKS.
Every BOARD can be evolved with the ARCADE MACHINE. It will require 1 STEP COIN per step.
The TOP EVOLVED NFTS at the snapshot time will qualify for the PHASE 1 AIRDROP.
